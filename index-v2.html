Seattle Changed the Rules in 2025

Now is the best time in Seattle's history to build.

The city removed nearly every barrier that used to make ADU construction difficult.

  • Two ADUs now allowed per lot
  • No owner-occupancy requirement
  • Height limits raised to 32 feet
  • Permits in as little as 2–6 weeks
  • No parking requirements near transit
  • ADUs can now be sold separately with own title
